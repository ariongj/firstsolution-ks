Ask us directly
Do you take part in public tenders?

Yes. We prepare complete offers in line with public procurement requirements and deliver exactly to the technical specification and terms of the contract.

Is your equipment original and under warranty?

Yes. We supply genuine equipment from HP, Dell, Lenovo, Cisco and other leading brands through official channels, with full manufacturer warranty.

Do you install and support what you deliver?

Yes. Our team in Prishtina handles installation and configuration, and supports you on-site and remotely throughout the warranty period and beyond.

Can you advise us before we buy?

Of course. We can assess your current setup and recommend the right hardware, network and licensing for your needs and budget.

Do you work outside Prishtina?

Yes. We serve clients across Kosovo, and we are expanding toward Albania and North Macedonia.
